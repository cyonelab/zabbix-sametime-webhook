# Code of Conduct

## Our standards

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, provide constructive feedback, and focus discussions on improving the project.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and comments that do not follow these standards. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately through the organization's published contact channel.
